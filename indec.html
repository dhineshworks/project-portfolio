Marketplace E-Commerce Platform

SoftSync Marketplace is a prototype e-commerce platform designed for small business owners to sell both physical and digital products online. The platform allows business owners to create accounts, manage products, and reach customers through a centralized marketplace. Customers can browse products, place orders, and manage their purchases securely. The system includes role-based access for customers, business owners, and administrators. Built with modern web technologies, it provides a scalable foundation for future marketplace growth and advanced business features.

Technical Details

SoftSync Marketplace is a full-stack marketplace application built using **React, TypeScript, Vite, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB**. It uses **JWT authentication and role-based access control** to manage Customers, Business Owners, and Admins securely. The platform supports product management, image uploads, order processing, and digital product sales through a scalable REST API architecture.
